## LinkedIn Caption

I just launched a live demo of MediPilot AI, a full-stack AI clinical workflow assistant built for doctors and small clinics.

This project is not a landing page. It is a working healthcare productivity platform with:

- doctor, clinic admin, and assistant roles
- patient records, consultations, notes, tasks, follow-ups, and documents
- AI consultation summaries, SOAP drafts, task extraction, risk flags, referral drafts, semantic search, and patient-friendly summaries
- a patient portal for clinic requests and reviewed updates
- AI governance with review status, provider/model metadata, audit logs, and clear safety disclaimers
- PostgreSQL, Prisma, Redis/Valkey-compatible caching, Docker, CI/CD, and deployment-ready docs

For the public demo, AI runs in a free safe fallback mode, but the architecture supports local Ollama and provider adapters. Every AI output is treated as a draft and requires doctor review.

The biggest goal was to show production-minded engineering: not just UI screens, but auth, RBAC, database design, auditability, deployment readiness, and clinical safety boundaries.
